| 2. No informational-only keyword under 500 volume | Applied to keywords whose Semrush intent is *only* "Informational" | 18,405 dropped |
| 3. Informational-only: KD 0–15 and high volume | KD ≤ 15 **and** volume ≥ 500 | 2,260 dropped for KD |
| 4. Commercial / navigational / transactional: KD 0–25, volume 20+ | Includes mixed intents such as "Informational, Transactional" | 1,602 dropped for KD, 138 for volume |

Two more decisions:

- **Product head terms Semrush labels "Informational"** (e.g. *eye fillet*, *gravy beef*, *lamb mince*) go on the **product page**, not a blog post — Google shows shopping results and product pages for these. Only recipe / "how to" / "vs" / "what is" searches go to the blog.
- **Relevance filter:** a keyword can pass your numbers and still be worthless to you — e.g. *1915 lanzhou beef noodles* (6,600), *coles mince* (2,400), *beef tallow moisturiser*, *a frame chicken coop*. These were removed (counts in section 9) because ranking for them brings no orders.

## 3. Strategy — what drives sales, in priority order

1. **Win the product head terms (biggest volume, lowest difficulty).** 72 product pages get a primary keyword. Eye fillet, scotch fillet, sirloin/porterhouse, oxtail, beef tongue, lean mince, chicken feet, beef liver and lamb mince alone are ≈72,000 searches/month at KD 9–24. Each page gets a keyword-led title, H1, a unique 150–250-word description, a price/delivery answer in the first 100 words, and a 2–3 question mini-FAQ.
2. **Own "halal" + "near me" through Google Business Profile.** *halal butcher near me* (5,400), *halal meat near me* (3,600), *halal meat shop near me* (1,900) are answered by Google's map pack. The homepage targets them, but **a Google Business Profile (service-area, Greenacre) is what actually ranks in that pack** — this is the single most valuable owner task.
3. **Build a dedicated Wagyu page.** 49 Wagyu keywords (≈11,800/mo — *wagyu steak* 2,400, *australian wagyu* 1,000, *wagyu near me* 880) currently have no single home; you stock 23 Wagyu products. Proposed: a Wagyu landing page linking to every Wagyu product.
4. **Category pages capture the "buy" searches.** Lamb (*lamb* 12,100, *lamb prices*), Goat (*goat meat near me*, *goat meat*), Duck, Camel, Kangaroo, Buffalo, Chicken (*halal chicken*).
5. **Offal and bones are an under-served niche you already stock.** Tongue, oxtail, liver, chicken feet/necks/frames, marrow and soup bones total ≈30,000 searches/month at KD 8–24 — many buyers are pet owners and soup/broth cooks; few competitors target them.
6. **Blog posts that sell.** 38 topics, led by "scotch fillet vs porterhouse/eye fillet" (≈10,000/mo), slow-cooked beef cheeks (≈5,800), lamb casseroles, chicken maryland recipes. Every post links to the product it features.
7. **Fill product gaps worth adding** (section 7): kangaroo mince (2,400), beef/veal schnitzel (1,990), skirt steak (≈6,000), beef tallow (≈9,600).
